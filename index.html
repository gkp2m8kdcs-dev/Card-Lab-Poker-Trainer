Card Lab: Poker Trainer

Poker Training Studio · v1.0

An offline study and training companion for card-game enthusiasts. Build your own decks, log the hands you see in practice, and drill the patterns that make you sharper at the table.

100% Offline No Account No Tracking No Real Money Local-First

What you can do

🂠

Custom Decks

Choose built-in presets — Classic Spade, Hearts Edition, Forest Clubs, Royal Violet — or design your own with a monogram, color palette, or a photo of your own card back.

🗒️

Hand Logger

Capture every study hand: cards, seat position, table size, outcome, notes, and tags. Build a personal record of how your practice evolves.

🧠

Trainer Drills

Four interactive drills: Memory Drill, Hand Rankings, Odds Lesson reference, and a 20-hand Speed Game to test your reflexes.

📊

Weekly Stats

See how your study time is spent — hand-rank distribution, outcome mix, and weekly activity trends for self-coaching.

🔒

Your Data, Your Device

Everything stays on your iPhone. No login, no analytics SDKs, no servers. Card Lab is a journal and study tool — nothing more.

Important — No Real-Money Gaming

Card Lab: Poker Trainer is a study and training app. It contains no chips, tokens, currency, or wagers of any kind. It does not connect to any casino, card room, betting service, or sportsbook. There are no in-app purchases related to gambling and no way to spend real money inside the app.

The hand-ranking lessons, odds tables, and drills included with the app are standard poker-theory references intended for educational use only.
