Support & Help

Card Lab: Poker Trainer · v1.0

Thanks for using Card Lab: Poker Trainer. This page answers the most common questions about the App. If you can't find what you need below, please email us — we read every message and reply as quickly as we can.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Card Lab: Poker Trainer include any real-money gambling?

No. Card Lab: Poker Trainer is a study and training app only. It contains no chips, tokens, currency, wagers, or in-app purchases of any kind. It does not connect to any casino, card room, betting service, or sportsbook. The hand-ranking lessons, odds tables, and drills are standard poker-theory references for educational use.

Is an account or internet connection required?

No. The App does not require you to register, sign in, or provide any contact information. All features work fully offline. The App does not communicate with any server during normal use.

Where is my data stored?

All data — custom decks, photos, hand log entries, tags, and notes — is stored locally on your device inside the App's sandboxed container. Nothing is uploaded to a server. If you back up your device to iCloud or a computer via standard Apple tools, those backups are governed by Apple's privacy policy.

How do I delete a deck or a hand entry?

Open the deck or hand you want to remove and look for the delete / trash control. Confirm the prompt, and the item will be removed immediately from your device. To remove everything at once, delete the App from your device — this permanently removes all App data.

Why was the camera or photo library permission requested?

The App asks for Camera access only when you tap "Take Photo" for a custom card back, and for Photo Library access only when you tap "Choose from Library." Both are optional — you can deny either permission and still use every other feature of the App.

Can I use my own photo as a card back?

Yes. In the deck editor you can either take a new photo with the camera or pick one from your library. The image is stored locally and used as the back design for that deck.

Do I need to pay to unlock features?

Card Lab: Poker Trainer v1.0 includes all features in the initial download. There are no in-app purchases required to use the Decks, Logger, Trainer, or Stats tabs.

How do I report a bug or request a feature?

Please email us at ondrickaamber7121998@gmail.com with a short description of the issue, your iOS version, and your device model. For feature requests, tell us what you'd like to see and how it would help your study workflow.

How do I reset the App to a clean state?

Because everything lives on your device, deleting and reinstalling the App will give you a completely fresh installation with no decks, no hand history, and no custom photos. Be sure you have exported or written down anything you want to keep first.

Tips for a Smooth Experience

Tag and pin your favorite decks

Use tags to organize decks by game (e.g. "Spades-only", "Hearts-only") and pin the decks you use most so they appear at the top of your Decks list.

Use the Notes field liberally

The hand-log Notes field is freeform — perfect for marking tells, table dynamics, or anything you want to remember when reviewing past hands.

Mix drills for best results

Combine the Memory Drill (short-term recall) with the Hand Rankings reference (theory) and the Speed Game (reflex ranking) for a balanced study session.
