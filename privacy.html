Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 16, 2026 · Last Updated: October 16, 2026
No Account No Tracking No Data Collection Local-Only

This Privacy Policy explains how Card Lab: Poker Trainer (the "App", "we", "our") handles information when you use it. By using the App you agree to this Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

Short version: Card Lab: Poker Trainer does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data. Everything you create in the app — decks, hands, photos, tags, stats — is stored only on your device and never leaves it.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Card Lab: Poker Trainer is designed from the ground up to be a local-first app. The App does not collect, store on a server, transmit, share, or sell:

  • Personal identifiers (name, email, phone, address, account credentials).
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs (IDFA, GAID, etc.).
  • Location data (precise or coarse).
  • Usage analytics or behavioral tracking.
  • Crash logs, diagnostics, or performance telemetry sent to external services.
  • Photos, images, or notes you create inside the App.
  • Hand entries, deck configurations, tags, or any study content.

The App does not require you to register, sign in, or provide any contact information to use any of its features.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

All user-generated content is stored locally on your device using Apple's standard, sandboxed storage frameworks. Specifically:

DataStored WherePurpose
Custom decks (names, colors, monograms, tags, notes) On-device file system (App sandbox) Display your decks inside the App
Card-back photos taken or imported by you On-device file system (App sandbox) Render your custom card backs
Hand log entries (cards, position, outcome, notes, tags) On-device file system (App sandbox) Show your history and compute local stats
App settings and preferences On-device standard preferences Remember your choices between launches

This data stays inside the App's sandboxed container. It is never uploaded to our servers (we do not operate any servers for the App), nor shared with third parties.

3. Permissions

The App requests the following iOS permissions only when you choose to use the related feature:

  • Camera — used only when you tap "Take Photo" to create a custom card-back image. The photo is saved inside the App's sandbox. You can deny this permission; in that case you can still import photos from your library instead.
  • Photo Library (read-only) — used only when you tap "Choose from Library" to import a custom card-back image.
  • Haptics — used for subtle tactile feedback during training. No data is collected.

The App does not request microphone, contacts, location, motion, advertising, or background-tracking permissions.

4. Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party SDKs or services. This includes, without limitation:

  • No analytics SDKs (Firebase, Amplitude, Mixpanel, etc.)
  • No advertising networks or ad mediation SDKs
  • No crash reporting or remote logging services
  • No social-login or share SDKs
  • No backend API calls of any kind

5. Children

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. Because no personal data is collected from anyone, this Policy satisfies applicable children's privacy laws (including COPPA) by design. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has used the App, you may contact us to ask about any locally stored content on your device.

6. Data Deletion & Your Controls

Because your data is stored only on your device, you have complete control over it:

  • You can delete any deck, hand entry, or custom photo at any time from inside the App.
  • Deleting the App from your device permanently removes all of its sandboxed data.
  • If your device is backed up to iCloud or your computer via standard Apple backup features, those backups are governed by Apple's privacy and security policies — not by this App.

7. International Users

The App is intended for use worldwide. Because the App does not transmit personal data to any server, no cross-border data transfer occurs. Regardless of where you live, your data remains on your device.

8. Changes to This Policy

If we ever change this Policy, we will update the "Last Updated" date above and, where required by applicable law, prompt you within the App to review the new version before continuing. Because we do not collect identifying information, we cannot notify you by email.
